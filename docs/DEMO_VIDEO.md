# 30-second demo video: script and shot list

Length: 30 seconds or less. English subtitles are required: use `demo_subtitles.srt`. Record the screen of the live app (1920 x 1080 or 1280 x 720), no AI-generated footage. No NASA logos.

Tip: open the app with the shareable links below so each shot starts in the right state. Record each shot separately and cut them together.

| Time | What is on screen | Voice and subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Title slide (`slides.html#1`), team names visible | "We are Sudais and Sunnia, team TERRAPULSE, from Topi, Pakistan." |
| 0:03 to 0:05 | Story chapter 3 "Clouds can't hide the water": drag the divider from left to right | "During the monsoon, clouds blind optical satellites. NISAR's radar sees straight through." |
| 0:05 to 0:10 | Story chapter 1, then press Play on the pulse strip; the map animates through the year | "TerraPulse: reading Earth's signals, revealing its changes." |
| 0:10 to 0:20 | Story chapter 6 (rice in standing water, purple fields), then Explore: drag a box to measure an area, the chart appears | "A year of NISAR radar shows rice fields flooding, growing and being harvested: twelve hundred square kilometres of rice standing in water." |
| 0:20 to 0:27 | Switch site to the Amazon at Manaus, then NISAR worldwide with "Find NISAR passes" | "From the Indus to the Amazon, anyone can measure, compare and download the change." |
| 0:27 to 0:30 | Title slide or app with the address on screen | "Next: early flood alerts for Pakistan, and for any river NISAR sees." |
