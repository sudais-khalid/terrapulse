# Code of Conduct

TerraPulse adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's *Security* tab (**Report a vulnerability**) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
